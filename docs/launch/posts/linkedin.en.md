## Post

Most retros lose their first ten minutes to sign-ups and sticky-note logistics.

I built RetroBoard to remove that: one link, no account, and the team is writing together. Boards are end-to-end encrypted, the relay stores nothing and there are no trackers.

What you get:
- **Real time** boards for the whole team
- **Private drafts**, published in one click
- **Groups and votes**, secret if you want
- **Shared timer** and **Format suggestions**
- **Exports** to Markdown, PDF, Slack and Jira

It is built in public. The source is available under the PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 license (not open source), so you can read it, check it and self-host it for noncommercial use.
