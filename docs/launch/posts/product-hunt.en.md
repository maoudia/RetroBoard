## Name

RetroBoard

## Tagline

Retros people look forward to, with no account and end-to-end encryption

## Description

A real-time retrospective board. One link and the team is in. Boards are end-to-end encrypted, nothing is stored on a server, and there are no trackers.

## Maker comment

Hi Product Hunt, I built RetroBoard because retros should start in seconds, not after everyone signs up.

- **Real time**: every note, vote and move shows up for everyone.
- **Private drafts**: write without influencing others, then publish in one click.
- **Groups and votes**: stack notes, vote with a limited budget, secretly if you want.
- **Shared timer**: the same countdown for everyone.
- **Format suggestions**: tell us the goal and the time you have, we suggest the format.
- **Exports**: Markdown, PDF, Slack, Jira.

It is built in public and source available under the PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 license. It is not open source: read it, check it and self-host it for noncommercial use.
