## What it is

RetroBoard is a real-time retrospective board. One link and the team is in: no account, no email, no password.

## Privacy by construction

- Boards are end-to-end encrypted. The key lives in the link fragment and never reaches the relay.
- The relay stores nothing. The board stays on your devices, encrypted, as long as your retention setting allows.
- No analytics, no cookies, no third-party scripts, self-hosted fonts.

## What you can do with it

**Real time** boards, **Private drafts** that leave your browser only when you publish, **Groups and votes**, a **Shared timer**, **Format suggestions** and **Exports** to Markdown, PDF, Slack and Jira.

## Built in public

The code is built in public and source available under the PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 license. It is not open source: you can read it, check what it does and self-host it for noncommercial use.
