## First comment

Hi HN. RetroBoard is a real-time retrospective board: one link and the team is in, with no account to create. Boards are end-to-end encrypted, the key stays in the link fragment, and the relay stores nothing.

What it does: **Real time** boards, **Private drafts** you publish in one click, **Groups and votes**, a **Shared timer**, **Format suggestions** and **Exports** (Markdown, PDF, Slack, Jira).

It is built in public and source available under the PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 license (not open source): you can read it, check what it does and self-host it for noncommercial use. There are no trackers, no cookies and no third-party scripts.

- Try it: https://retroboard.maoudia.com/
- Source: https://github.com/maoudia/RetroBoard
- User guide: https://retroboard.maoudia.com/guide/en/
