# Facilitator role with delegation

## Context and Problem Statement

Phases, timers, talk time and vote reveal need someone who drives them.

## Considered Options

* Facilitator role with delegation to co-facilitators
* Everyone equal

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: "Facilitator role with delegation to co-facilitators", because one person must own the flow of a retro.

### Consequences

* Good, because clear control of the session
* Bad, because roles must be verified by every peer (signed events)

## More Information

Clarified by the single-facilitator change (one lead at a time).
Refined by ADR-0021 (everyone facilitates by default).
