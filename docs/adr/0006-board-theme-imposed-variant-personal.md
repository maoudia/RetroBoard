# Board theme imposed, light or dark personal

## Context and Problem Statement

Participants could override the board theme, so a team never shared one ambience.

## Considered Options

* Theme imposed by the facilitator, light or dark variant per participant
* Personal theme override

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: "Theme imposed by the facilitator, light or dark variant per participant", because the product owner wants one shared ambience and variants keep accessibility.

### Consequences

* Good, because everyone sees the same board
* Bad, because a participant who dislikes a theme can only switch its variant
