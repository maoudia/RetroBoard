Scrum Poker is a room for estimating together: everyone picks a card, nobody sees the others, and the cards turn over at once when the moderator reveals them. It is in beta: stories, a timer and exports are not there yet. There is no account, and the room is encrypted like a board: the link carries the key after the `#`, so the server only ever sees ciphertext and keeps nothing.

## start
Find **Scrum Poker** on the home page, in the boards menu or on the library page. Pick a deck (Fibonacci, modified Fibonacci, T-shirt, powers of 2) or type your own cards separated by commas, for example `1, 2, 4, 8, ?`: they show as round chips. Then press **Create the room**.

## room
Type a name and join: that is all that is asked. You are the moderator. **Copy the link** (or show the QR code, or use Share on a phone) and send it to the team: a person who opens it types a name and sits at the table. Your hand is a row of playing cards at the foot of the screen, in two rows on a phone (a long custom deck is one row you slide sideways). Pick a card by tapping it or by typing its name on the keyboard (`5`, `1` then `3` for 13, `?`): the card you chose is lifted, ringed and checked, and you can change it until the reveal. The table shows who has voted, never what. The moderator presses **Reveal**: your hand gives way to the results and every card turns at once, with the average, the median, the most chosen card, the spread, the agreement and a suggested card. **Vote again** starts another round on the same item, **Next round** a fresh one. Reloading the page keeps your seat and your card. A room holds 20 voters; later arrivals watch.
