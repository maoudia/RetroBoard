## participants
The participant list shows who is online, who is typing right now and in which column, and everyone's role. By default everybody can facilitate; the options let you keep a single facilitator.

The facilitator can rename a participant, hide their new notes, or remove them. A removed person leaves the board and sees a page that says so. Because anyone who still has the link could read what comes next, the removal offers **Move the team to a fresh link**: a new room and key are created, the board is kept as it is (notes, authors, votes), and each person present is sent the new link, sealed to them, so the team lands on the new board by itself. The removed person never receives it, and the old link opens no board. Anyone who was offline during the move is not sent the link: send it to them with Share.

## chat
The board chat lets people talk without leaving the retro. Like everything else it is encrypted and disappears with the board.
