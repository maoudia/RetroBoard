## board
Each column has its own field. What you write stays a **private draft** on your device that nobody else sees. Publish when you are ready: the idea appears for every participant. You can group similar ideas by dragging one onto another (on a touch screen, press and hold the idea until it lifts, then drag it; the idea's menu also has "Group with..."), and vote with the thumb.

A banner lets you undo a publication made by mistake for a few seconds.
