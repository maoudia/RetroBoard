## form
Open the creation page, give the board a title (for example "Sprint 24") and pick a retro format: Start, Stop, Continue, Went well, Sailboat and many more. A passcode is suggested by default: it protects access to the board on top of the link.

## theme
The facilitator picks the theme and it applies to the whole team. Everyone keeps the choice between the light and the dark variant for themselves.
