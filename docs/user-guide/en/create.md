## team
In advanced mode, choose your team first, at the top of the creation page. In simple mode the row is left out and the board belongs to your last team. Pick a team (or "New team" and give it a name) and, if you like, a project. The team's last format, theme, agenda and the next title of its series then prepare the form below. A title you already typed or a format you already picked is never replaced. If you leave the row alone, the board belongs to the last team you used, with no project, as before. The carry-over of open actions, in advanced mode, reads the team and project you chose.

## form
Open the creation page, give the board a title (for example "Sprint 24") and pick a retro format: Start, Stop, Continue, Went well, Sailboat and many more. In simple mode the page is short: the title, a password made for you (copy it and send it apart from the link), the theme, the format and a switch for the timed agenda, which is on by default. If your team has open actions from its last retro, one switch brings them to the new board. Ambient effects, solo mode, the presenter menus, the voting and facilitation choices and your own password are in advanced mode. In advanced mode you can choose or generate the password. In advanced mode, a board belongs to a team: that team starts its next board with its last format, theme and agenda, and proposes the next number of the series ("Sprint 24" becomes "Sprint 25"). With two teams or more, a team switcher appears above your recent boards on the home page and in your profile. Pick a team to show only its boards; it also becomes the team of your next board. "All teams" shows everything again. Teams stay on this device: nothing about them is sent to a server. To bring a team to another device, open your profile, then "Share a team": copy the encrypted team link or download the .retroteam file. It carries the team name, look and settings, never a board, a note or an action. On the other device, open the link or the file and the team joins your list. Your boards are kept in a library on this device, sealed with a key only your browser holds: open "My boards" (from your profile or the board switcher) to reopen, archive or forget one. The boards you create join it by themselves; a board you only joined by link stays out until you press "Save to my library" (or turn on "Save boards I join" in your profile).

## theme
The facilitator picks the theme and it applies to the whole team. Everyone keeps the choice between the light and the dark variant for themselves.
