## form
Open the creation page, give the board a title (for example "Sprint 24") and pick a retro format: Start, Stop, Continue, Went well, Sailboat and many more. A passcode is suggested by default: it protects access to the board on top of the link. In advanced mode, a board belongs to a team: that team starts its next board with its last format, theme and agenda, and proposes the next number of the series ("Sprint 24" becomes "Sprint 25"). With two teams or more, a team switcher appears above your recent boards on the home page and in your profile. Pick a team to show only its boards; it also becomes the team of your next board. "All teams" shows everything again. Teams stay on this device: nothing about them is sent to a server.

## theme
The facilitator picks the theme and it applies to the whole team. Everyone keeps the choice between the light and the dark variant for themselves.
