## drawer
The decisions of the retro become actions: a text, an owner and a due date. An action can come from an idea on the board, and the open actions of one retro can be carried over to the next.

## celebrate
When an action reaches done, the idea it came from, and the action in the plan, play a short animation of the board theme: confetti, a stamp, a ghost, drifting petals... It lasts about a second, never blocks what you are doing, and everybody who has the board open sees it at the moment it happens; a board you open later stays calm. With reduced motion, a check mark shows for a moment instead. In advanced mode, the profile has a switch "Celebrations when an idea is done" to turn it off for you only.
