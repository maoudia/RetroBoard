Invite the team

The board link carries the encryption key: share it, that is all.

1 The Share panel gives the link, the code and the QR code.

The Share panel offers three ways to invite: the link (paste it in a chat or an e-mail), the QR code to scan with a phone, and a short code to type on the home page. The three board words let everyone check out loud that they are on the same board.

Do not post the link in a public channel: anyone who has it can read the board.
