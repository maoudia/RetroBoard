User guide

RetroBoard is a real-time retrospective with no account: one link, and the team is on the board. This guide follows a retro from creating the board to exporting it.

The screenshots come from the application itself, on a demo board with invented names and ideas. The buttons at the top of each page switch the language and the light or dark variant.

Everything stays private. The board is end-to-end encrypted: the key is in the link after the # and never reaches our servers. Nothing is stored on a server, the board lives in the participants' browsers.
