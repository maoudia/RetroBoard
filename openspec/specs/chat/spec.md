# chat Specification

## Purpose
The live chat drawer: roomy on phones, with the participants strip folded by default and a messaging-style composer.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Roomy phone chat
On phones the chat SHALL take most of the screen and keep the participants strip folded by default.

#### Scenario: Open chat on a phone
- **WHEN** the chat opens on a small screen
- **THEN** the strip is folded and the sheet is tall
