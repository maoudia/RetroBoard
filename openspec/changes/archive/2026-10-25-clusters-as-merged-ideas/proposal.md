# Proposal

## Why

A group of notes is really one merged idea: it needs a name, it should be votable as one idea, and when a column has many groups people need to expand them all at once.

## What Changes

- Cluster title defaults to the first added idea's text and can be renamed; title suggestions from member texts (and from the local AI when enabled).
- Clusters are votable items (with `voting-v2-and-ranking`).
- "Expand all / Collapse all" per column, switching stacks to an inline expanded layout.
- Merge two clusters by dropping one on the other; split by dragging a note out.

Out of scope: cross-column clusters (kept per column).

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `grouping`: clusters as named, votable merged ideas, expand-all, merge.

## Impact

- Contexts: Ideation, Voting.
