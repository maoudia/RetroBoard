# Proposal

## Why

Owner feedback of 2026-11-04 (six phone screenshots): the retro is not yet linear by itself, the theme animation is hidden in advanced settings, the relay/direct explanation is technical, the vote pill overflows its stack, similar ideas are grouped poorly, the keyboard closes while stacking ideas on phones, the writing-size control has odd shadows and the voting menu still looks old.

## What Changes

1. Theme animation is on by default and a toggle at board creation turns it off (simple mode included).
2. Connection mode is explained with plain words and two distinct icons (direct between people, through the relay), no jargon.
3. The agenda is automatic by default: started at creation, phases chain by themselves when their planned time elapses unless the facilitator adds minutes, each change is announced.
4. The vote pill and the badges always stay inside the stack face.
5. Similar-ideas suggestion uses normalised words (accents, plurals, stop words) and weighted similarity.
6. Stacking ideas on a phone keeps the keyboard open.
7. Writing-size control: no stray shadows, same look as the other segmented controls.
8. Voting menu: remaining votes shown as tickets, fewer words.

### Modified Capabilities
- `retro-agenda`, `themes`, `sharing`, `voting`, `grouping`, `notes`, `ui-coherence`
