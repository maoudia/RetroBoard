# Proposal

## Why

Inviting the team must be one gesture. With E2E links (long), passcodes and presenter screens, sharing needs a dedicated, very simple flow.

## What Changes

- Share panel opens automatically for the facilitator on a new board, with one big "Copy invite" button.
- Invite message templates (Slack, Teams, email) with the link and, separately, the passcode.
- QR code (local generation) for in-room joining and presenter mode.
- Native share sheet on mobile.
- Short visual "room code" (words derived from the room id) to confirm people joined the right board (not a secret).

Out of scope: link shortener (would require storage).

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `sharing`: how a board is shared and joined quickly and safely.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

- Web share dialog, presenter mode; no server change.
