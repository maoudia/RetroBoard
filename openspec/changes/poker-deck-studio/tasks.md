# Tasks

Sequence (owner decision of 2026-11-21): this change starts after group 6 of `poker-design-v2` is merged (another worker is finishing it) and stays inside the standalone poker; `poker-in-retroboard` and `board-estimation-phase` come after it. The older changes' text is not rewritten ahead of the code: the pull request of a task amends what that task replaces (design, "What this replaces once it is implemented").

Order: what the owner sees first comes first. The cards themselves (group 1) go before the deck model (group 2), the builder (group 3) comes before the gallery (group 4) so that the typed list is gone as early as it can be and its replacement works the day it goes, and team decks and the in-room switch (group 5) and the gates (group 6) follow. One group is one pull request; a task is about two hours of one agent session. Every task keeps `pnpm e2e:core` green (the poker spec is in the core gate: the testids of an element that survives, such as `poker-card-<i>`, `poker-reveal`, `poker-seat` and `poker-hand`, stay) and lands its own tests and its i18n keys in fr and en. The guide chapter `poker` and the poker section of `docs/DEVELOPMENT.md` are corrected in the same pull request as any behaviour they describe (AGENTS.md). Screenshots of the result go in the pull request, never in the repository; `before/` and `concepts/` stay until the change is archived.

Verification vocabulary: "screenshot review" is a review of the real page at the sizes named against the wireframes of `design.md` and the sketches of `concepts/`, in light and dark, by the agent and again in the pull request; "axe" is axe-core in light and dark (`tests/e2e/helpers.ts`); "pseudo-locale" is `?lang=pseudo` (and French) at 360, 390, 768 and 1440 px; "bundle budget" is the entry and total budgets of `scripts/check-bundle-size.mjs` (entry 56.5 kB, total 500 kB), and this change adds at most 24 kB gzip in all and nothing to the entry; "token audit" is `apps/web/test/design-tokens.test.ts`. Group 1 of `poker-design-v2` (the card and the hand) is released, so `PokerCard` and `PokerHand` are changed here, not written.
