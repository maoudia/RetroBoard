# Proposal

## Why

Some people speak a lot, some never. Teams asked for a talk time feature: a per-speaker time budget with a visible ring or a start/finish popup, configurable in retro settings.

## What Changes

- Talk time setting: off, ring, popup.
- Facilitator (or the speaker, if allowed) starts and ends turns; a ring around the speaker's avatar shows the budget.
- Start and end notifications (popup or ring pulse, optional chime).
- Talk time distribution in the retro report.

Out of scope: audio detection of who is speaking.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `talk-time`: speaking turns with budgets and notifications.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

- Context: Talk time. Few events per retro.
