# Tasks

Order: what the owner sees first comes first, so the card and the hand (group 1) and the phone room in one viewport (group 2) go before the reveal, the larger screens, the start page and the gates. One group is one pull request; a task is about two hours of one agent session. Every task keeps `pnpm e2e:core` green (the poker spec is in the core gate: the testids of an element that survives, such as `poker-card-<i>`, `poker-reveal`, `poker-seat` and `poker-hand`, stay) and lands its own tests and its i18n keys in fr and en. The guide chapter `poker` and the poker section of `docs/DEVELOPMENT.md` are corrected in the same pull request as any behaviour they describe (AGENTS.md). Screenshots of the result go in the pull request, never in the repository; `before/` stays until the change is archived.

Verification vocabulary used below: "screenshot review" is a review of the real page at the sizes named against the wireframes of `design.md`, in light and dark, by the agent and again in the pull request; "axe" is axe-core in light and dark (`tests/e2e/helpers.ts`); "pseudo-locale" is `?lang=pseudo` (and French) at 360, 390, 768 and 1440 px; "bundle budget" is the entry and total budgets of `pnpm prepush`.
